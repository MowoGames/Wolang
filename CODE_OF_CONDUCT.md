# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to make something good, as
you are.

## What is expected

- Talk about the work, not the person.
- Say what is wrong with a change and what would fix it. "This is wrong"
  helps nobody; "this drops the lock before the write, so two threads can
  interleave here" helps everybody.
- Take disagreement about code as what it is: two people with different
  information. Ask what the other one knows.
- Remember that most people here are not writing in their first language,
  and that a blunt sentence is often a translated one.

## What is not

Harassment, insults, and personal attacks. Sexualised language or
imagery. Publishing someone's private information. Sustained disruption
of discussion. Advocating for or encouraging any of the above.

## Where this applies

Everywhere the project is: issues, pull requests, discussions, and
anywhere someone is representing the project.

## If something goes wrong

Report it to <mowogames@proton.me>. What you send stays between you and
the maintainer. You will get an answer, and you will be told what was
done.

The maintainer will remove comments, commits, issues and contributions
that do not belong here, and will say why. Repeated or severe behaviour
means a ban.
